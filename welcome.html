Step 3 of 5

Connect WhatsApp Web

WAMarketer works inside WhatsApp Web, so it sends from your own number.

  1. Open WhatsApp Web in this browser.
  2. Link your phone: in WhatsApp on your phone, go to Settings → Linked devices → Link a device and scan the QR code.
  3. Find the sidebar on the right. Switch between CRM and Marketing at the top of it.
  4. Keep the tab open while campaigns run. It can stay in the background.

Step 5 of 5

You're ready to launch

Your first campaign in 4 moves

  1. Add recipients: upload a CSV or paste numbers with the country code.
  2. Write the message: use variables like {{name}} and attach an image or PDF if you like.
  3. Send a test to your own number and check how it looks.
  4. Start sending with the safe random delay, daily limit and sending hours left on.
Only message people who agreed to hear from you. Messaging strangers who report you can get a WhatsApp number banned.
